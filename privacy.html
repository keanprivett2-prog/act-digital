Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

At ACT Digital, we respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, request a quotation or contact us.

1. Who We Are

ACT Digital is a South African digital services business operating under the trading name ACT Digital. We provide website development, digital marketing and related services.

For privacy-related enquiries, please contact: enquiries@actdigital.co.za .

2. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or quotation request, we may collect:

  • Your name and surname
  • Your business or company name
  • Your email address and telephone number
  • Your website address, if provided
  • Your estimated project budget
  • Details about your project or enquiry
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

We may also collect technical and website usage information through analytics technologies, including browser information, device details, pages visited, traffic sources and interactions with our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests
  • Prepare proposals and discuss potential projects
  • Communicate with prospective and existing customers
  • Provide and improve our digital services
  • Understand website traffic and performance
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We process personal information where there is an appropriate lawful justification, such as responding to your request, taking steps towards a contract, complying with the law or pursuing legitimate business interests where permitted.

4. Firebase Firestore

ACT Digital uses Google Firebase Firestore to store information submitted through website enquiry and quotation forms.

This information is used to manage enquiries, prepare quotations and communicate with customers. We take reasonable steps to restrict unauthorised access to stored information.

5. Resend and Email Communications

We use Resend, a third-party email service provider, to process and deliver enquiry-related emails and notifications.

Information included in an enquiry may be processed by Resend to deliver relevant email communications. Enquiries may also be received and handled through our business email address.

6. Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website and to help improve its performance and content.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about website visits, device and browser characteristics, interactions and approximate location.

Depending on the configuration of Google Analytics and your privacy choices, information may be processed by Google in other countries.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small files or identifiers used by websites and related technologies. Our website may use cookies required for functionality and analytics cookies to measure website performance.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Where required, we will provide appropriate choices regarding non-essential cookies.

8. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our website, manage enquiries or provide services.

These providers may include Google Firebase, Google Analytics, Resend and our email hosting provider.

9. International Processing

Some service providers may process or store information outside South Africa. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that international transfers comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

10. Information Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access and unlawful processing.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete, destroy or de-identify it, where appropriate.

12. Your Rights Under POPIA

Subject to applicable legal requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold your personal information
  • Request access to personal information we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally appropriate
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
  • Submit a complaint to the Information Regulator

You can contact the South African Information Regulator through its official website: inforegulator.org.za .

13. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies or legal obligations change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.

15. Contact Us

For privacy questions, access requests, corrections or deletion enquiries, please contact:

ACT Digital
South Africa
