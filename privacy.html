Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Introduction

ACT Digital respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through the ACT Desk waiting list.

2. Information We Collect

When you join the waiting list, we collect your email address, consent confirmation, and registration timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address to send ACT Desk launch announcements, product updates, and early access information.

4. Consent

Joining the waiting list requires your agreement to receive these communications. You may withdraw your consent at any time.

5. Storage and Security

Waiting-list information is stored using Supabase. We use access restrictions, security verification, and other safeguards to help protect submitted information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell waiting-list email addresses. Service providers may process information on our behalf to operate the waiting list and deliver communications.

7. Retention

We retain waiting-list information while it is needed for launch communications and related purposes. You may request removal of your information at any time.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw consent.

Unsubscribe or Remove Your Information

If you no longer wish to receive ACT Desk launch updates, you can request removal from our waiting list.

Request removal from the waiting list

We will review your request and remove your email address from the waiting list after verifying the request.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests to unsubscribe or remove your information, contact enquiries@actdigital.co.za .

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as ACT Desk develops. The latest version will be available on this page.
